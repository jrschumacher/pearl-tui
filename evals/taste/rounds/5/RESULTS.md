the white, one had blue, one had a purple button, one had underline button. But if you
think about it pragmatically, it doesn't change the experience." Each session ran the
`brand` order and derived its own theme, so what the order didn't fix (the button form,
an accent's hue) drifted between them; the structure the orders and § Screens fixed (the
journey column, the route, the review, every state) did not.

kept every `keep:` line, each held by a test. What the person said in the interview
reached a session that never saw it, through the work orders. A detail the person cares
to keep (a button form) belongs on a `keep:` line or in § Brand read, or it is the next
session's to choose.
