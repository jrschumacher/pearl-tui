# What the skill's rules should produce (written before any build or the grader's answer)

## tallyho
- job: the week is browse + a live timer; invoice is checkout-like (money) but nothing is
  paid or sent: export can be redone, so no confirm, export is the act.
- carry: the running timer (the product's purpose) is a one-row strip on every screen,
  including the invoice, with project and elapsed time ticking.
- data: entries by day, hours right-aligned; the day total and week total accented.
- invoice: a receipt: line items, hours × rate right-aligned, total last in the accent,
  period and client on their own line; export button names the act ("Export PDF").
- brand: tally mark one row beside the name; no tall mark.

## burrow
- job: browse (inbox) with a reading pane beside the list (two panes), mouse on: wheel
  scrolls the pane under the pointer, ←→ only for wide lines in the message.
- background: sync is a glyph in the header/footer status, details on a help key;
  words only while offline AND the user is waiting (e.g. send queued).
- carry: nothing big; maybe unread count.
- compose: send is rung 2 (composed then sent): no confirm, 5 s undo toast.
- brand: quiet; the door mark small; "never shouts": no bold unread wall, unread by a
  glyph/weight, not colour floods.

## nestkeeper
- job: configure (settings): sections + list of settings + the selected setting's detail
  in one region beside the list; search by name.
- carry: pending-changes count always visible (e.g. "3 pending · a review & apply").
- review & apply: old → new per change; the admin-port change flagged (can lock you
  out); apply is the act the flow exists for → focus on Apply, BUT the lock-out change is
  rung 3/4-ish: a signal → the question names it. Safe button "Back to settings" /
  "Keep current", never bare Cancel.
- apply result: partial failure shows which change failed and why, others applied.
- brand: nest mark one row; calm, no colour floods.
