# burrow

A terminal email client for people who read a lot of mail and answer some of it.
Synthetic fixture: every product, person and brand in it is fictional.

## Brand
- Name: lowercase `burrow`, always.
- Colours: lantern rose `#E3B5A4` (primary) and slate `#7F8FB3` (secondary) on midnight
  `#0F1220`.
- Logo: a round burrow door with a small warm light inside (describe it; there is no
  file).
- Voice: cozy, unhurried, private. Never an inbox that shouts.
